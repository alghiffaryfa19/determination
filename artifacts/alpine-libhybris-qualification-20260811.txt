Embedded-linker TLS lifetime
----------------------------
Android bionic DSOs use Initial-Exec TLS. In the embedded linker there is no
normal PT_INTERP lifetime, so unloading only part of a local load group first
CHECK-aborted in unregister_tls_module and then, when only the TLS DSO was
pinned, left dangling dependency graph state and segfaulted after dlclose.

The final adapter pins a static-TLS DSO and propagates NODELETE to its complete
local load-group root. Real in-guest tests, with coherent /proc and namespaces:

  getprop ro.build.version.sdk -> 36, rc=0       PASS
  20 consecutive loader/property cycles         PASS
  post-HWC-restoration property query -> 36      PASS
